- [Sketchfab Blender Addon](https://github.com/sketchfab/blender-plugin): Browse, import, and upload 3D models directly between Sketchfab and Blender. `[open-source]`
- [Substance 3D add-on for Blender by Adobe](https://substance3d.adobe.com/magazine/the-substance-3d-add-on-for-blender-is-here/): Load and tweak parametric Substance `.sbsar` materials directly inside Blender.
- [Daz to Blender Bridge](https://www.daz3d.com/daz-to-blender-bridge): Transfers Genesis characters and rigs from Daz Studio directly into Blender.
- [LibSM64-Blender](https://github.com/libsm64/libsm64-blender): Integrates `libsm64` physics and Mario 64 character workflows into Blender. `[open-source]`
- **Format Importers**: [Daz Importer](https://bitbucket.org/Diffeomorphic/import_daz), [Rhinoceros 3D (.3dm)](https://blenderartists.org/t/import-rhinoceros-3d-files-into-blender/1136070), [XPS/XNALara](https://github.com/johnzero7/XNALaraMesh)

### Export & Format Converters

- [Export Paper Model from Blender](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/export-paper-model/): Unfolds 3D meshes into printable SVG/PDF papercraft patterns. [[docs]](https://addam.github.io/Export-Paper-Model-from-Blender/) `[ext:export-paper-model]` ([Repository](https://github.com/addam/Export-Paper-Model-from-Blender))
- [Batex](https://github.com/jayanam/batex): Batch-exports selected objects as FBX compatible with Unity's coordinate and scaling system. `[open-source]`
- [Export Object Dimensions](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/export-object-dimensions/): Exports width, height, depth, and volume of selected objects to TXT, CSV, or JSON with unit conversion. `[GPL · ext:export-object-dimensions]` ([Repository](https://github.com/rafaeloliveiraz/blender-dimexport))
- **Format Exporters**: [Bezier2SVG](https://leomoon.com/store/plugins/leomoon-bezier2svg/), [Panda3D (blend2bam)](https://github.com/Moguri/blend2bam), [X-Ray Engine](https://github.com/PavelBlend/blender-xray)

### Photogrammetry & Gaussian Splatting

- [3DGS Render by KIRI Engine](https://github.com/Kiri-Innovation/3dgs-render-blender-addon): Open-source add-on for importing, editing, deforming, and rendering 3D Gaussian Splatting (`.ply`) scenes directly inside Blender. `[GPL]`
- [Gauss Cannon](https://github.com/warpgatelabs/gauss-cannon): Utilities for generating and managing 3D Gaussian Splats directly from Blender scenes. `[GPL]`
- [Gaussian Splat to Nodes](https://github.com/gokayfem/gaussian-splat-to-blender-nodes): Toolkit for reconstructing 3D Gaussian Splats as editable Geometry Nodes meshes and shader setups in Blender. `[MIT]`
- [Photogrammetry Importer](https://github.com/SBCV/Blender-Addon-Photogrammetry-Importer): Imports reconstruction outputs from COLMAP, Meshroom, OpenMVG, and VisualSFM into Blender. `[open-source]`
- [blenderkitti](https://github.com/risteon/blender_kitti): Renders large LiDAR point clouds and voxel grids in Blender. `[open-source]`

### Geographic & Maps

- [BlenderGIS](https://github.com/domlysz/BlenderGIS): Bridges Blender with geographic data (shapefiles, DEM elevation, satellite imagery, and OpenStreetMap). `[open-source]`
- [Maps Models Importer](https://github.com/eliemichel/MapsModelsImporter): Imports 3D city captures from Google Maps into Blender. [[more info]](https://blog.exppad.com/article/importing-actual-3d-models-from-google-maps) `[open-source]`
- [blender-osm](https://gumroad.com/l/blender-osm): One-click download and import of OpenStreetMap buildings and terrain for Blender.

### Web, XR & Collaboration

- [Needle Engine](https://engine.needle.tools/docs/blender/): Creates interactive, lightweight Three.js / WebXR web applications directly inside Blender.
- [Verge3D](https://www.soft8soft.com/get-verge3d/): Toolkit for creating interactive 3D web experiences and product configurators from Blender scenes.
- [Multi-user](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/multi-user/): Enables real-time multi-user collaborative scene editing inside Blender. [[thread]](https://blenderartists.org/t/multi-user/1187118) `[ext:multi-user]` ([Repository](https://gitlab.com/slumber/multi-user))

## Workflow, UI & Utilities

### Asset & Scene Management

- [BlenderKit](https://www.blendkit.com/): Download 3D models, PBR materials, HDRIs, and brushes directly into Blender.
- [FontSelector](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/font-selector/): Visual font browsing and management inside Blender. `[ext:font-selector]` ([Repository](https://github.com/samytichadou/FontSelector_blender_addon/))
- [Simple Renaming Panel](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/simple-renaming-panel/): Batch-rename objects, bones, materials, and data-blocks with prefix/suffix and regex rules. `[ext:simple-renaming-panel]` ([Repository](https://github.com/Weisl/simple_renaming))
- [Lodify](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/lodify-optimizer/): Level of Detail (LOD) and proxy mesh management system for Blender. `[GPL · ext:lodify-optimizer]` ([Repository](https://github.com/DB3D/Lodify))
- [PowerSave](https://gumroad.com/l/powersave): Streamlined incremental saving and autosave management for Blender artists.

### Viewport, Nodes & UI

- [Modifier List](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/modifier-list-fork/): Enhanced compact UI layout, sidebar tab, and popup menu for managing modifier stacks. [[thread]](https://blenderartists.org/t/modifier-list-for-blender-4-2-addon-fork/1512291) `[ext:modifier-list-fork]` ([Repository](https://github.com/Symstract/modifier_list))
- [Copy Attributes Menu](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/copy-attributes-menu/): Adds a `Ctrl+C` popup menu to copy transforms, modifiers, constraints, and properties between objects. `[GPL · ext:copy-attributes-menu]`
- [Amaranth](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/amaranth/): Studio productivity tweaks, render helpers, and UI shortcuts maintained by Blender Studio. `[GPL · ext:amaranth]`
- [Screencast-Keys](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/screencast-keys/): Displays keyboard and mouse inputs on-screen for tutorials and demos. `[ext:screencast-keys]` ([Repository](https://github.com/nutti/Screencast-Keys))
- [Node Pie](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/nodepie/): Fast pie menu for searching and adding new nodes in the Shader and Geometry Node editors. `[ext:nodepie]` ([Repository](https://github.com/strike-digital/node_pie))
- [NodeToPython](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/node-to-python/): Converts Geometry, Shader, and Compositing node trees into clean, reusable Python scripts and add-ons. `[MIT · ext:node-to-python]` ([Repository](https://github.com/BrendanParmer/NodeToPython))
