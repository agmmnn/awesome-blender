## 3D Models

## Mocap/Animation

### Free Mocap & Animation Libraries

- [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com/): Free auto-rigging and thousands of downloadable character animations for games, film, and Blender workflows.
- [Quaternius Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html): Free game-ready humanoid animation pack distributed in clean `FBX` and `GLB` formats. `[ZERO]`
- [CMU Motion Capture Database](https://sites.google.com/a/cgspeed.com/cgspeed/motion-capture): 2,500+ free human motion capture animations in `.bvh` format from Carnegie Mellon University.
- [ACCAD Open Motion Project](https://accad.osu.edu/research/motion-lab/mocap-system-and-data): Open motion capture archive (`.bvh` / `.c3d`) from Ohio State University covering locomotion, martial arts, and everyday motions.
- [Bandai Namco Research Motiondataset](https://github.com/BandaiNamcoResearchInc/Bandai-Namco-Research-Motiondataset): Large stylized human motion-capture dataset released by Bandai Namco Research for personal and academic use. `[open-source]`
- [AMASS](https://amass.is.tue.mpg.de/): Large-scale archive unifying 15+ optical motion capture datasets (including CMU) into a standard SMPL human skeleton representation.
- [Rokoko Studio](https://www.rokoko.com/products/studio/download): Includes 150+ free animations in Rokoko's Motion Library alongside Blender retargeting workflows.
- [ActorCore](https://actorcore.reallusion.com/free): Motion-capture animation library from Reallusion featuring free starter packs and commercial assets.
- **turbosquid/motion-capture / Sketchfab/animated**: [turbosquid/motion-capture](https://www.turbosquid.com/Search/Motion-Capture/free/), [Sketchfab/animated](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models?features=downloadable+animated&sort_by=-likeCount)

### AI Video-to-Mocap & Markerless Capture

- [FreeMocap](https://freemocap.org/): Free, open-source markerless 3D motion capture studio using standard cameras/webcams with built-in Blender scene and rig export. `[open-source]` ([Repository](https://github.com/freemocap/freemocap))
- [GVHMR](https://github.com/zju3dv/GVHMR): Gravity-aware AI 3D human motion recovery from single-camera monocular video for animation and SMPL/Blender pipelines. `[open-source]`
- [DeepMotion](https://www.deepmotion.com/): Cloud AI video-to-mocap tool capturing full-body, hand, and facial motion with `FBX`, `BVH`, and `GLB` exports for Blender. `[paid]`
- [Move.ai](https://www.move.ai/): Markerless single- and multi-camera AI motion capture with production `FBX` export. `[paid]`
- [Plask](https://plask.ai/en-US): Browser-based AI video-to-mocap and keyframe cleanup platform exporting `FBX` and `BVH` animations. `[paid]`
